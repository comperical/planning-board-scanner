# Sandown, NH — Planning Board Web Access

Investigated: 2026-09-14

Rockingham County town. Platform: standard CivicPlus Agenda Center (see
PATTERNS.md) — no bot protection. Has a direct per-board Agenda Center
URL.

## Platform notes

- Main site: `https://www.sandown.us`. Planning Board Agenda Center:
  `/AgendaCenter/Planning-Board-10/`.
- Like Raymond, files individual **"Site Walk Agenda"** documents tied to
  a specific property (e.g. "7.7.26 Planning Board Site Walk Agenda for
  412 Main Street") separate from regular meeting agendas — a useful
  direct-address signal source.
- Meetings: 1st & 3rd Tuesday monthly, 6:30pm, Town Hall.
